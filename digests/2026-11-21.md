## 1. Bridging vision-language reasoning and learned control for legged navigation in the wild (ROAM) · 5.52
Takahiro Miki, Robin Schmid, Manthan Patel, Turcan Tuna, Julia Richter, Julian Nubert, Joonho Lee, Matthias Müller, Marco Hutter (ETH RSL), Science Robotics
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25215 · project: https://roam-hiking.github.io · code: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/roam
Tag: **Must-read** · Pick: taste · Evidence: real robot (legged, kilometer-scale alpine + urban hikes)
Why read: A VLM reads the scene (risks, signs), open-vocabulary segmentation turns its labels into a dense real-time
risk map, and that map conditions two sim-trained RL policies (agile locomotion + local navigation). Kilometer-long hikes
with minimal intervention. This is the Miki/Lee/Hutter perceptive-locomotion lineage with semantics added on top.

## 2. PROOF: Perceptive Robustness via Out-of-Distribution Fallback for Legged Locomotion · 5.22
Siyuan Wang, Dongjie Zhu, Jinxuan Lu, Wenjun Xu, Jiankun Wang, CoRL 2026
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18305 · code: none found
Tag: **Must-read** · Pick: taste · Evidence: real robot (Unitree Go2 + D435) · Your rating: 👍
Why read: Teacher-student perceptive locomotion. The student predicts exteroceptive features with uncertainty and suppresses them on
OOD inputs (occlusion, reflections, bad lighting), falling back to proprioception. It bridges proprioceptive and exteroceptive control, which is where your work is heading.

## 3. ReLoco: Unified Humanoid Locomotion and Fall Recovery via Behavior-Conditioned Motion Priors · 5.04

## 4. From Temperature-Limited to Battery-Limited Legged Locomotion · 4.80 (new)
Raphaël Ségas, Balázs Opra, Léo Ghafari, Thomas Stewart
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22231 · dataset: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24274994
Tag: **Must-read** · Pick: taste · Evidence: real robot (Unitree Go2, 10 km runs, stairs + ramps)
Why read: A fitted 13-node RC thermal model predicts all 12 motor temperatures (<1 °C RMSE). The reward penalizes the hottest
*predicted steady-state* temperature, which reacts within one gait cycle, so the policy learns to spread heat without ever
observing temperature. With a 5 kg payload it ran 174 min / 10 km, while the baseline hit thermal shutdown at 19 min. This is reward design you can reuse.

## 6. SBEE: Proprioceptive Simultaneous Base and Terrain Elevation Estimation for Legged Robot Locomotion · 4.56
Michael Maravgakis, Stylianos Piperakis, Panos Trahanias
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24310 · code: billed as open source (no link yet)
Tag: **Must-read** · Pick: taste · Evidence: sim (MuJoCo quadruped + biped) + ANYmal-D GrandTour dataset · Your rating: 👍

## 7. SG-ODOM: Event-Driven Proprioceptive State Estimation with Stability-Gated Contact Updates for Legged Robots · 4.38 (new)
Francesco Iotti, Chiyun Noh, Franco Angelini, Manolo Garabini, Ayoung Kim, Maurice Fallon, Frank Dellaert (Oxford DRS et al.)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20710 · code: https://github.com/ori-drs/SG-ODOM
Tag: Skim · Pick: taste · Evidence: real-robot sequences (quadruped + humanoid platforms, GrandTour)
Why read: Leg odometry assumes a stance foot is stationary, but impact and load transfer break that. SG-ODOM accepts a
contact only once a stability gate says the foot is planted, then fires one event-driven correction. It gets up to 25% lower RPE
on outdoor GrandTour sequences. Same theme as SBEE, and it has code.
